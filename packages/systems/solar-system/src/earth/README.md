# Earth & Luna

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known body in the universe to harbor life. This implementation includes both Earth and its natural satellite, Luna (the Moon), with accurate physical, orbital, and atmospheric properties.

## Scientific Context

### Earth's Unique Characteristics

- **Only Known Life**: Earth is the only body in the universe confirmed to harbor life
- **Liquid Water**: Abundant surface water in all three phases
- **Active Geology**: Plate tectonics, volcanism, and seismic activity
- **Magnetic Field**: Protective magnetosphere generated by core dynamo
- **Atmosphere**: Nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere supporting complex life

### Geological Composition

- **Core**: Iron-nickel inner and outer core
- **Mantle**: Silicate rock with convection
- **Crust**: Continental and oceanic plates
- **Hydrosphere**: Oceans, lakes, rivers, and groundwater
- **Atmosphere**: Nitrogen (78%), oxygen (21%), trace gases

### Luna's Characteristics

- **Synchronous Rotation**: Always shows the same face to Earth
- **Ancient Surface**: Heavily cratered from early bombardment
- **No Atmosphere**: Direct exposure to space
- **Geologically Dead**: No active volcanism or tectonics
- **Regolith**: Fine dust covering the surface

### Celestial Object Properties

#### Earth

- **Type**: `CelestialType.PLANET`
- **Planet Type**: `PlanetType.TERRESTRIAL`
- **Composition**: Silicates, iron core, liquid water, nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere
- **Atmosphere**: Nitrogen-oxygen with blue glow effect

#### Luna

- **Type**: `CelestialType.MOON`
- **Planet Type**: `PlanetType.BARREN`
- **Composition**: Silicate rock, iron core, no atmosphere
- **Surface**: Heavily cratered, regolith-covered

### Surface Properties

#### Earth Surface

- **Type**: `SurfaceType.VARIED`
- **Color Palette**: Blues, greens, browns, whites
- **Terrain**: Oceans, continents, mountains, plains
- **Atmospheric Effects**: Blue sky glow, cloud scattering

#### Luna Surface

- **Type**: `SurfaceType.CRATERED`
- **Color Palette**: Grays and browns
- **Terrain**: Highlands, maria, craters, regolith
- **No Atmosphere**: Direct space exposure

## Technical Notes

### Coordinate System

Earth orbits the Sun at 1 AU with a slight eccentricity, while Luna orbits Earth at approximately 384,400 km. The Earth-Luna system's barycenter is located within Earth's interior.

### Physics Integration

- **Mass**: Used for gravitational calculations in N-body simulations
- **Radius**: Determines collision detection and visual scaling
- **Atmosphere**: Affects lighting and visual rendering
- **Tidal Effects**: Earth-Luna gravitational interactions

### Simulation Considerations

- **Life Support**: Earth's atmosphere and temperature support life
- **Magnetic Field**: Earth's magnetosphere protects from solar radiation
- **Tidal Locking**: Luna's synchronous rotation with Earth
- **Orbital Stability**: Earth-Luna system stability over geological time

## Exploration History

### Earth Exploration

- **Human Civilization**: Continuous habitation for thousands of years
- **Scientific Study**: Comprehensive understanding of all systems
- **Space Exploration**: Launch site for all human space missions
- **Environmental Monitoring**: Global climate and ecosystem studies

### Luna Exploration

- **Apollo Program**: 6 manned landings (1969-1972)
- **Robotic Missions**: Multiple orbiters and landers
- **Sample Return**: 382 kg of lunar material returned
- **Future Plans**: Artemis program for renewed exploration
