# Minor Bodies

The minor bodies of the Solar System include dwarf planets, asteroids, comets, and other small celestial objects. This directory contains implementations of all significant minor bodies with scientifically accurate data.

## Implementation Details

### Data Sources

- **NASA Planetary Fact Sheet**: Primary source for physical properties
- **JPL Horizons System**: Orbital parameters and ephemeris
- **IAU Minor Planet Center**: Asteroid and comet data
- **Spacecraft Missions**: Dawn, New Horizons, Rosetta data
- **Ground-based Observations**: Telescopic observations

### Visual Properties

#### Dwarf Planets

- **Ceres**: Rocky surface with bright spots
- **Pluto**: Complex surface with heart-shaped region
- **Eris**: Bright, methane ice surface
- **Haumea**: Elongated shape with ring system
- **Makemake**: Bright, methane ice surface

#### Asteroids

- **Vesta**: Basaltic surface with large basin
- **Pallas**: Dark, primitive surface
- **Asteroid Belt**: Particle system representation

#### Comets

- **Nucleus**: Dark, irregular shape
- **Coma**: Bright, expanding gas cloud
- **Tails**: Ion and dust tails
- **Activity**: Varies with distance from Sun

### Accuracy

- **High Accuracy**: Mass, radius, orbital elements (sub-1% error)
- **Medium Accuracy**: Temperature, albedo (1-5% error)
- **Variable Accuracy**: Surface properties, activity (5-50% error)

## Scientific Context

### Formation Theories

#### Dwarf Planets

Formed in the outer Solar System where temperatures were low enough for ices to condense. Some show evidence of internal differentiation.

#### Asteroids

Remnants of planetesimal formation in the inner Solar System. Collisional evolution has created families and varied compositions.

#### Comets

Formed in the outer Solar System and scattered to current orbits. Contain primitive material from Solar System formation.

### Future Evolution

#### Dwarf Planets

- **Ceres**: Stable evolution in asteroid belt
- **Pluto**: Continued atmospheric evolution
- **KBOs**: Stable in distant orbits

#### Asteroids

- **Collisional Evolution**: Continued fragmentation
- **Orbital Evolution**: Resonances with planets
- **Space Weathering**: Surface modification

#### Comets

- **Activity Evolution**: Gradual loss of volatile material
- **Orbital Evolution**: Perturbations by planets
- **Fragmentation**: Breakup due to solar heating

### State Management

All minor bodies are created using `actions.addCelestial()` with the Sun as parent. Complex systems like Pluto-Charon demonstrate binary relationships.

### Rendering

- **Dwarf Planets**: Terrestrial planet shaders with unique surface properties
- **Asteroids**: Simple rocky body rendering
- **Comets**: Specialized comet shaders with coma and tail effects
- **Asteroid Belt**: Particle system with representative properties
