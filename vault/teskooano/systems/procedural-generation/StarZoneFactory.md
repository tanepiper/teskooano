# StarZoneFactory

Builds star-specific zone templates for diverse stellar types (e.g., white dwarfs, neutron stars, black holes, giants, spectral classes).

## Behavior

- Scales base AU ranges and adjusts allowed planet types by stellar context
- Applies interstellar/frozen/cold/cool/hot/temperate zone patterns per type
