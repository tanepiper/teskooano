## Release Highlights

- New physics system build from the ground up with new algorithims and integrators
- New multi-star lighting system that provides real colour and luminocity based on star type, celestial albedo and distance
- New multi-shadow system that casts multiple shadows on celestials from moons, and casting shadows on ring systems
- New comet celestial type that has a coma and tail system that reacts to the distance and position of the star their orbit
- New information panels for celestials that give information about light, shadows, gravity and other details
- Improved travel camera and notification system
- New celestial labels with distance and speed along with the celestial name
- New AU labels that go out to 100,000 AU - the labels also react to celestials so disappear to have less clutter on the screenshot
- Prediction and orbit line improvements, with prediction labels showing the time a celestial will take to travel
- Improved procedural solar system generation

... and many more improvements.
